The workshop series

About EMAS

Bringing together the communities that design, build, and study multi-agent systems.

EMAS began in 2012 at the Dagstuhl seminar on Engineering Multi-Agent Systems. It united three established workshops and their research communities:

  • AOSEAgent Oriented Software Engineering
  • DALTDeclarative Agent Languages and Technologies
  • ProMASProgramming Multiagent Systems
