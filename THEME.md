# Theme provenance

This site uses Michael Rose's **So Simple 3.2.0** Jekyll theme, downloaded from:
https://github.com/mmistakes/so-simple-theme/tree/3.2.0

The original `_sass`, base `_layouts`, `_includes`, and theme assets are bundled locally so the page does not rely on a remote-theme download. The upstream MIT licence is retained in `LICENSE-theme.txt`.

Customisations: `workshop` layout; masthead, navigation, and footer includes; workshop and person includes; `head-custom.html`; relative local script URLs in `scripts.html`; `assets/css/workshop.css`; and `assets/js/workshop.js`. Content is in `index.html` and `_data`.

The EMAS logo and factual records originate from https://emas.in.tu-clausthal.de/ (retrieved 23 November 2026). The latest edition's date and venue were checked against https://emas-workshop.github.io/2026/. Logo and workshop content are not relicensed by the theme's MIT licence. Existing bibliographic entries and historical external URLs are retained.
